# Prize2Pride Short-Form Learning Design

## Product Position

The creator-led feed is not a social-media clone. It is a mobile-first **practice loop** that borrows the immediacy of short-form interfaces while treating every interaction as a bounded learning unit: notice a Portuguese phrase, hear it, respond to it, receive corrective guidance, and save or schedule the next review.

## Beginner-to-Hero Journey

| Stage | Learner promise | Content rhythm | Evidence of progress |
|---|---|---|---|
| Beginner | Understand and respond in a first real-life situation | One phrase, one listening cue, one choice | First completed moments and first saved phrase |
| Explorer | Navigate familiar daily moments | Vocabulary recall plus guided response | Situation streak and saved practice set |
| Communicator | Sustain useful exchanges | Context card, response, and repair practice | Repeated scenario mastery |
| Confident | Adapt register and context | Compare responses and choose a better phrase | Independent scenario completions |
| Hero | Help others and perform in high-stakes contexts | Challenge, reflection, and tutor-guided retrieval | Verified capability milestones, never self-declared fluency |

## Short-Form Unit Contract

Each learning moment should remain compact and intentional. It has one **situation**, one **Portuguese target**, one **Arabic/Tunisian explanation option**, one **active response**, and one **next action**. The next action is always learner-visible: save, replay, practise with a tutor, or complete a linked lesson. Endless autoplay, opaque engagement ranking, public follower counts, and compulsive scroll mechanics are intentionally excluded.

## Creator Governance

Tutor-authored content and future creator submissions must expose authorship, language labels, source lesson or situation, proficiency level, review state, and an easy correction report. Only reviewed creator material may appear in default learner recommendations. Generated text is a draft until it passes curriculum and dialect-quality review; learner reports must be visible to the moderation queue.

## Research Note

The design favours short, active practice rather than passive consumption. Research on microlearning describes segmented learning units that can be delivered through text, video, audio, or activities, while short-video research also cautions that problematic use can undermine behavioural engagement.[1] [2] The initial sources were inaccessible to automated browser review because of anti-bot challenges, so these principles should be validated further with learner research and usability testing before broad release.
